Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

ManTen (六賽万点, "the game") is made by OWNER_NAME ("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you play, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, the display name you choose, and your password (stored only in scrambled form by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it).
  • Profile: your avatar (an emoji, or a photo you choose to upload), coins, level, experience points, the items you own and use, game stats (games played and won), and the players you've blocked.
  • Gameplay: the tables you join, your moves and scores, and chat messages you send at a table (each seat keeps its most recent messages).
  • Reports: if you report a player, we store the report, the reason, and their most recent chat lines so we can review it.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. We receive a record of what you bought (product, date, an anonymous purchase ID) through our purchase provider, RevenueCat, so we can give you the items.
  • Security data: to keep out bots and cheaters, Google services we use (Firebase App Check and reCAPTCHA) may process technical information such as your IP address and device or browser details.

Who can see what

Players at a table with you can see your display name, avatar, level, equipped cosmetics, scores and chat messages. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Why we use it

  • To run the game: accounts, tables, turns, scores, chat, coins and items.
  • To keep it fair and safe: stopping cheating, bots and abuse, and reviewing reports.
  • To deliver what you buy and restore it on a new device.
  • To send account emails you ask for (confirming your email, resetting your password).

Services we use

Google Firebase (accounts, database, server functions, security checks), Apple and Google (purchases and, in the app, notifications), RevenueCat (purchase records), and GitHub Pages (hosting this website). They process data only to provide those services.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and profile until you delete your account. Chat messages are replaced as new ones are sent. Reports are kept for up to one year. Purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account

In the game, open the menu (☰), go to Account and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account, profile, coins, levels and items. You can also email us at CONTACT_EMAIL and we'll do it for you. Purchases made through Apple or Google can't be refunded by us; see their refund policies.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data. Email CONTACT_EMAIL and we'll help.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

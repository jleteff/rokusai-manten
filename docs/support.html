Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation email. Check your spam or junk folder. On the "Check your email" screen, tap Send it again.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link in the email.

Someone is being abusive. Tap their seat, then Report or Block. If you're the host, you can also remove them from the table.

A purchase didn't show up. In the app, open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If it's still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

I want a refund. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (your order history).

How do I delete my account? Menu (☰) → Account → Delete account.

How do I play? Open the menu (☰) in the game and tap How to play.
